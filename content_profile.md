# K2 Digital Media — Content Profile

Use this profile to (1) score which stories are worth posting and (2) shape the carousel copy.

## Creator

- Brand: **K2 Digital Media** — a full-service digital agency in London, Ontario, Canada.
- Author / byline: **Keerthivasan**
- Instagram: **@k2digitalmedia_**
- Services: web development & design, video & VFX, digital marketing & SEO, and IT.

## Niche

K2 Digital Media posts practical, trend-aware content that positions the agency as the local
expert for small and mid-sized businesses. Core topics, in priority order:

1. **Web development & design** — front-end, UX, accessibility, performance, modern CSS/JS,
   no-code vs custom, site speed, conversion.
2. **Digital marketing & SEO** — Google algorithm updates, local SEO, content strategy,
   social/video marketing, analytics, PPC, AI in marketing.
3. **Video & VFX** — editing/motion-graphics tooling (DaVinci, After Effects, Premiere),
   short-form video strategy for brands.
4. **Tech & business trends** — launches, funding, and platform changes that a local business
   owner should care about (trend-jacking, framed for relevance).

**Avoid:** hard political news, crime, celebrity gossip, or anything that can't be turned into
a useful takeaway for a business owner or marketer.

## Audience

Primarily **business owners, marketers, and founders in London, Ontario** and nearby
Southwestern Ontario — plus anyone evaluating a digital agency. They are time-poor and want
the "so what" fast. Make local relevance explicit when a story is national/global.

## Tone

Clear, practical, locally aware, confident, and direct. Lead with value, avoid hype, never
pad. Every slide should give the reader a reason to keep swiping.

## Content Rules

- Lead with value before context.
- Keep the CTA to one clear action (follow / DM / save).
- Make local (London ON) relevance explicit when it is not obvious.
- Explain *why the story matters* to a business owner — no vague summaries.
- Do not invent facts beyond the source story.
- Scoring bias: a story scores higher when it maps cleanly onto one of the four niche topics
  AND can be made actionable for the audience.
